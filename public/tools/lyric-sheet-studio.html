Lyric Sheet Studio

Paste your own words, pick a column layout and your iPad size, and the page auto-sizes the type to fill one screen. Everything runs locally in this tab — nothing you type leaves your browser. When it looks right, press and hold the rendered image to save it.

Blank line = new verse/chorus block. Start a line with * for chorus styling. Wrap words in **double asterisks** to bold them — handy for marking who sings which part.
